# Launch and discoverability

The build contains crawlable HTML on each route. JavaScript is used for enhancement rather than to provide the main page content.

Before indexing, configure the real domain and consistent business name/address/phone. Rebuild after every data change. Inspect canonical URLs and sitemap URLs in out/. The default example.com URLs must not be used for a production launch.

Use truthful, useful service content and explicit answers to customer questions. Organization and Service JSON-LD describe the brand and services. FAQPage matches the visible FAQ content; its presence does not guarantee a Google FAQ rich result. Do not add review ratings or LocalBusiness addresses before they are genuine. Once a real business location is supplied, extend the Organization schema to the appropriate LocalBusiness subtype with matching visible address, telephone and opening hours.

For geographical discovery, confirm the actual service area and add unique location-specific content only for places genuinely served. Avoid mass-generated doorway pages. Register and maintain the business profile where eligible and keep contact information consistent.

For answer engines, keep important answers in the initial HTML, describe limits and assumptions, and reference primary sources for changing programme rules. llms.txt is optional descriptive text, not a recognized guarantee of indexing or citation. robots.txt allows crawlers but cannot force them to visit.

After launch, submit the sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, inspect indexing, check real mobile Core Web Vitals, and monitor actual search queries. No ranking, traffic, savings or AI recommendation is guaranteed.
